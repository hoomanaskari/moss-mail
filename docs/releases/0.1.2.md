Email links open in Moss, and Undo after a delete works every time.

- **Make Moss your default email app.** In Mail, choose Settings, then General, and pick Moss as the Default email reader. Then an email link on a website or in another app opens a new email in Moss, with the address, subject and text already filled in. It works whether Moss is open, hidden or closed.
- **Email links in your mail open in Moss too.** Clicking an email address inside an email starts a new email in Moss instead of in another app.
- **Safer sending.** Moss uses a newer version of the part that sends your email, with fixes for several security problems found in it this week.
- **Undo after deleting an email.** Sometimes an email you brought back with Undo was moved to Done a moment later. It now stays in your Inbox.
