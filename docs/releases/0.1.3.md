Forwarded emails open ready to read.

- **Forwarded emails show what was forwarded.** Some forwards, such as ones sent from Apple Mail in a language other than English, opened almost empty until you clicked "Show quoted text". Now the first email of a conversation always shows the email it quotes, because you have nowhere else to read it.
- **Forwarded conversations stay short.** When someone forwards a whole conversation, the newest email in it is open and the older ones wait behind "Show quoted text".
