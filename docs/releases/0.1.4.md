Deleted emails can come back, and emails sent to a crowd stay tidy.

- **See what you deleted.** Trash is in ⌘K now. It lists the emails you deleted, newest first, and you can open one to read it.
- **Put an email back.** In Trash, press E (or click Back to inbox) and the email returns to your inbox, even one you deleted from Done. It moves out of Trash on your mail server too.
- **Long recipient lists are short again.** An email to forty people used to stretch the line under the sender far past the reading column, with a sideways scroll. Now it names three people, you first, and counts the rest ("to you, Lena, Jonas and 37 others"). Click it to see everyone.
- **The reading column keeps to its width.** Anything too wide for it now shrinks or ends in "…" instead of scrolling sideways.
